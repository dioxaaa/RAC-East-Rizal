ABOUT US

Who we are

The Rotaract Club of East Rizal brings together young professionals and students who believe in leading through service. Sponsored within the Rotary International family, we run local projects in health, education, environment, and livelihood — while building lifelong friendships and leadership skills along the way.

Service

Hands-on community projects that respond to real, local needs across East Rizal.

Leadership

Every member takes on real responsibility — planning, organizing, and leading initiatives.

Fellowship

A close-knit club culture built on friendship, mentorship, and shared purpose.

Global Connection

Part of a worldwide network of Rotaractors working toward common goals.
